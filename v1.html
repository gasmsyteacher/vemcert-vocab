Vemcert Vocabulary

Version 1 · 48 terms used in the credentialSubject and evidence of W3C Verifiable Credentials issued with Vemcert.

https://w3id.org/vemcert/v1#

Stability

This version is immutable. Terms may be added. A term’s meaning is never changed and a term is never removed: term IRIs are signed into credentials meant to verify indefinitely, offline, after the issuing organization or the platform no longer exists. Breaking changes mint a new version path (/v2).

Scope

Only domain-specific terms are minted here. Person-level properties use established vocabularies in each credential type’s own context: holderName is schema:name, dateOfBirth schema:birthDate, placeOfBirth schema:birthPlace, thesisTitle dc:title. A term carries an @type only where Vemcert itself checks the value’s form; values written by a registrar (awardDate, session) stay untyped rather than assert a format the data does not honour.

Academic and professional awards

Evidence

TermRangeDescription
AttendanceEvidenceuntypedClass. Evidence that a claim was derived from an attendance record whose eligibility was computed by an upstream system.
LegacyMigrationuntypedClass. Evidence that a credential re-issues an award recorded by an earlier system; its validFrom is the original award date.
basisuntypedThe rule or source a claim rests on: for attendance cpd, hours or attendance; for a migration, the kind of earlier system.
computedBy@idIdentifier of the system that computed eligibility. An HTTPS origin, not a DID: it asserts nothing on its own.

hoursAttendedxsd:decimalHours of attendance the computation was based on. Absent when unknown, never null.
recordedAtxsd:dateTimeWhen the evidence was taken from the upstream system — not when the activity took place.
formulaVersionxsd:integerVersion of the formula the upstream system used to compute eligibility.

Machine-readable context

The JSON-LD context is served from the same IRI under content negotiation (Accept: application/ld+json) and at v1.jsonld. Credentials embed these definitions inline in their @context; nothing dereferences this document when a credential is issued or verified, which is what lets a credential verify offline from a printed QR code. In this document the namespace is bound to the prefix vemcert:; in issued credentials to certificate:. Both expand to the same IRI, and the IRI is what is signed.
