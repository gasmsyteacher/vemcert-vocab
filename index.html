Cachet Vocabulary

Terms used in the credentialSubject and evidence of W3C Verifiable Credentials issued by Cachet.

Versions

  • Version 1 — https://w3id.org/cachet/v1# (JSON-LD context)

Published versions are immutable: terms may be added, never redefined or removed. Term IRIs are cryptographically signed into credentials that are intended to remain verifiable indefinitely — including offline, after the issuing institution or this platform no longer exists.
